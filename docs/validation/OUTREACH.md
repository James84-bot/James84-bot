# Finding and booking interviews

Goal: 10 interviews in three weeks. Expect to contact about 30 businesses to
get 10 yeses.

## Where to find them

- Your own network first: anyone who supplies to, buys from, or works at a
  hardware shop, wholesaler or distributor. Ask each person for two more names.
- Walk-ins at quiet times (mid-morning Tuesday to Thursday, not month-end):
  Industrial Area and Kirinyaga Road (Nairobi), Thika town, Nakuru CBD.
- Accountants and bookkeepers who serve SMEs. One bookkeeper can introduce
  several clients and is also a useful interview in their own right.
- Trade groups and WhatsApp groups for hardware dealers and agro-dealers. Ask
  the admin before posting.

Aim for the mix in VALIDATION.md §4: at least six businesses with two or more
branches or a separate store.

## First message (WhatsApp or SMS)

Fill in the brackets. Have a native speaker check the Swahili version before
first use.

**English**

> Hello [name], I'm [your name] from JMH Automation. I'm doing short research
> on how wholesalers and hardware suppliers manage stock, supplier orders and
> customer credit. I'm not selling anything. Could I have 30 minutes with you
> this week, in person or by phone? Your answers stay private and I won't use
> your business name.

**Kiswahili**

> Habari [jina], naitwa [jina lako] kutoka JMH Automation. Ninafanya utafiti
> mfupi kuhusu jinsi wafanyabiashara wa jumla na maduka ya hardware
> wanavyosimamia stock, oda kwa wasambazaji na madeni ya wateja. Sina kitu cha
> kukuuzia. Je, naweza kupata dakika 30 kuzungumza nawe wiki hii, ana kwa ana
> au kwa simu? Majibu yako yatabaki siri na sitatumia jina la biashara yako.

**Follow-up (after 3 days, once only)**

> Hello [name], just following up on my message about the 30-minute chat on
> stock and supplier orders. If this week is busy, is there a better day?

## At the start of each interview (say this, don't skip it)

> Thank you for your time. I'm trying to understand how businesses like yours
> handle stock and orders, so I can decide whether a tool is worth building.
> There are no right answers, and it helps most if you tell me about real
> recent examples. I'll take notes but won't record your name, your customers'
> names or any phone numbers. Is it OK if I take notes?

If they say no to notes, take none and write up from memory straight after.

## Rules while interviewing

- Ask about the last time something happened, not what usually happens.
- Don't describe the product until question 10.
- Don't argue or correct. If they say "we don't have that problem", ask how
  they handled the last delivery or count, and listen.
- Ask the person who records stock separately from the owner. Their answers
  often differ.
- Write up notes in the template within 24 hours.

## After each interview

1. Save notes as `interviews/NN-<business-type>-<town>.md`, e.g.
   `interviews/03-hardware-thika.md`.
2. Fill in the scorecard in the note.
3. Add a row to `tracker.csv`.
4. Send a thank-you message the same day.
